🔥 Dynamic Thermal Safety & Fire Protection Cutoff

Monitors continuous temperature on heavy load lines (such as high-wattage water geysers and AC compressors). If prolonged overheat thresholds or short-circuit anomalies are sensed, the microcontroller executes an automatic hardware cut-off in under 10 milliseconds.

⚡ Smart Load Switching & Energy Telemetry

Utilizes ESP8266 and ESP32 low-power nodes communicating over ESP-NOW and MQTT. Dynamically regulates power consumption across peak hours, saving residential electricity and extending appliance operational lifespan.

📱 Remote WiFi & Local Mesh Redundancy

Supports dual-mode control: cloud telemetry for remote mobile access, with an automatic fallback to local peer-to-peer ESP-NOW mesh if external internet connectivity drops.
